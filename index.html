Manufacturing ERP
Multi-Plant Operations Suite
42% Faster Dispatch 58% Fewer Mismatches
Problem: Inventory and dispatch approvals were split across spreadsheets, email threads, and delayed sign-off loops.
Solution: Implemented one workflow-driven ERP with role approvals, stock validation checkpoints, and dispatch orchestration.
Outcome: Order-to-dispatch reduced by 42%, while stock mismatch incidents dropped by 58% in quarter one.
Field Service Mobility
Technician App + Dispatcher Console
2.3× Closure Speed 81% SLA Compliance
Problem: Service teams had no live view of ticket status, field location, and SLA breach risk.
Solution: Built a geo-tagged technician app with proof capture plus a dispatcher console for queue and escalation control.
Outcome: Ticket closure improved 2.3×, and SLA compliance increased from 46% to 81%.
